One page for the CFO, with the questions to settle before signing.

For whom first: the executive sponsoring a first India centre at a mid-market company.

Parent revenue US$100M to 1B, often PE-backed. 20 to 150 seats in year one. No India head hired yet. Has to convince a CFO or a PE board.

Why them

Most exposed to the information gap, least able to buy a Big 4 study, and growing: 480 to 583 mid-market GCCs.

Why not large enterprises

They already pay advisors, have real estate and tax teams, and buy on relationships.

Then

Existing GCCs adding a second city, then providers and states.

Business model

013

Free for the company deciding. Sellers pay for proof and permission.

YEAR 1

Verified provider profiles

Annual fee to show client-verified deliveries. Price to test: [₹__ per year].

YEAR 1

Consented introductions

A fee when an entrant chooses to share its plan with a provider. Price to test: [₹__ per intro].

YEAR 2

State and data products

Demand analytics for state agencies; registry licences for research and media.

Rules that protect neutrality, written into the product

Providers are listed alphabetically and can't pay to move. Paid profiles show more evidence, never a better rank. Kosh never recommends a provider. Intros happen only on the entrant's explicit choice, per provider. Every source's interest is shown.

Prices are deliberately blank: they are the first thing the 90-day test sets.

Risks

015

What could kill it, and what we do about it.

Small funnel

About 100 new GCCs a year. Widen to second-city expansions of 2,117 existing GCCs; the record serves both.

Cold start

Public data covers the tool on day one. Verified deliveries grow from real use.

Neutrality slips

Ranking can't be bought, written into the code and the provider terms.

Stale or wrong data

Every figure dated; dated figures marked. Human review before anything is shown. A public corrections log.

Legal: scraping, advice

No LinkedIn or Naukri scraping. Incentive checks labelled as screening, not legal advice.

Incumbents copy it

They can copy the screens, not the neutrality: they sell setup.

How I used AI

016

A lot, and checked every time.

Research and argument

Four parallel research agents on the market, players, entrant pain and data sources. I argued the strategy with it; it pushed back on my first pick.

What AI got wrong

A "US$12.15B GCC enablement market" kept appearing. It only existed on spam pages hosted on unrelated government domains. It's not in this case.

How the product keeps AI honest

It answers only from retrieved facts. Citations that don't exist are stripped. Numbers come from the engines, not the model. Nothing extracted is shown before a person approves it.

Every number has a source. That's the whole product.
